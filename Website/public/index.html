01 — IPHONE

Away from your Mac? Don't miss what needs you.

When a turn ends, fails, or gets interrupted, your iPhone gets a push. Tap through to the Session — ten seconds to decide whether to head back or keep waiting.

One iPhone pairs with multiple Macs; sync continues while the Mac app is closed, and the local cache stays browsable offline.

Read-only by design — you can see it, you can't change it. Currently available via TestFlight.

02 — NOTCH

At your Mac, a glance is enough.

Most of the time it's just a thin strip at the top. It expands only when a turn ends or fails — nothing in between.

Every interruption means you're actually needed.

03 — MAC

Called back? The full history is right here.

A three-column window — read Sessions the way you read email: who's waiting is obvious at a glance, the timeline shows exactly where it stopped, and tokens, context, and duration are one click away.

Your Session content is encrypted for each paired iPhone.

Relay forwards encrypted Session payloads — it stores nothing and reads nothing. The daemon on your Mac is the only source of truth; your iPhone keeps its own local cache.

FAQ

Which agents are supported?
The mainstream coding agents are supported today, with more to come.
Will it be noisy?
No. You're only notified when a turn ends, fails, or gets interrupted. Everything in between stays silent.
Does Lumi keep running when I close the window?
Yes. The Notch and sync continue to run.
Do I need an account?
No. Pairing uses a 6-character code plus a numeric comparison on both sides — no account system involved.
Can the iPhone control the agent?
No — v1 is deliberately read-only.
System requirements?
Apple silicon + macOS 26 on the Mac; iOS 26 on the iPhone.
